## Key claims
- **Publicar** (Free, Pro, Max): "Makes your artifact publicly available. Anyone with the link can view and interact with it."
- **Compartir** (Team, Enterprise): "Makes your artifact available within your organization only. Viewers must be logged into your Team or Enterprise account to access it."
- Un visitante sin cuenta puede ver un artifact publicado; sólo se le pide registro "for advanced features like using AI-powered capabilities".
- En Team/Enterprise los artifacts "can only be shared within your organization—they cannot be published publicly".
- Si el artifact nació en un Project: "if the artifact was created from a project, viewers must also have access to that project".
- Excepción Live Artifacts: "shared on Team and Enterprise plans only, within your organization, and can't be published publicly on any plan".
- Despublicar es de ida: "you cannot publish that same artifact again. You'll need to create a new artifact if you want to publish it later."
- El botón remix ya no existe; se puede copiar el código de un artifact publicado a un chat nuevo ("Your changes don't affect the original. You're working on your own copy.").

## Definitions and terminology
- **Publish vs Share:** publicar crea enlace público (planes individuales); compartir restringe a la organización (Team/Enterprise).
