## Key claims
- Los administradores pueden "distribute curated plugins to everyone in their organization" y "control exactly which plugins your team members can see and use".
- Cuatro opciones por plugin: "Installed by default" (instalado para todos, desinstalable), "Available for install" (autoservicio), "Not available" (oculto), "Required" (instalado automático y no removible).
- "Owners and Primary Owners of Team and Enterprise plans can manage organization plugins"; en Enterprise, el acceso se puede ajustar por grupo (gana la configuración más permisiva).
- "Members can't edit organization-managed plugins, which prevents conflicting changes to shared tooling."

## Definitions and terminology
- **Organization-managed plugin:** plugin cuya disponibilidad e instalación controla el administrador del plan Team/Enterprise.
