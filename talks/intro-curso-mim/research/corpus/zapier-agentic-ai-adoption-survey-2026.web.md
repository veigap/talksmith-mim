## Key claims
- Survey basis: Zapier surveyed 500+ enterprise leaders on how their companies develop, deploy, and invest in AI agents. Conducted by Centiment for Zapier, Oct 23–27, 2025; 525 completed surveys; respondents screened to be U.S. C-Suite Executives, Presidents, Owners, or Partners at companies with 1,000+ employees; data unweighted; margin of error ≈ ±4% at 95% confidence.
- **Adoption**: 72% of enterprises are now using or testing AI agents ("now have AI agents deployed and operating autonomously" — headline phrasing). Of this group: 40% have multiple AI agents in production; 32% are in pilot/testing. Another 14% plan to adopt even without agents today. Total: 86% of enterprises have AI agents in production, pilot, or planning phases.
- **How agents are built**: 53% use major cloud provider platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP); 48% use open-source tools; 48% use enterprise AI platforms; 46% use orchestration frameworks; 26% code custom agents from scratch. (Approaches are non-exclusive — many enterprises use multiple.)
- **Departments most likely to have deployed AI agents**: Customer Support 49%, Operations 47%, Engineering 35%, Marketing 31%, Sales 26%, Finance 24% (least likely).
- **Most popular AI agent use cases across departments**: data management (data entry/extraction) 47%; document analysis and summarization 41%; customer support triage and response 41%; report generation 36%.
- **Management approach**: human-in-the-loop remains the most common approach to AI agent management (38%) heading into 2026 — defined as building approval gates into AI workflows so a human reviews before output moves downstream. 20% (1 in 5) of enterprise leaders say their AI systems now operate autonomously with minimal oversight. Security and data privacy concerns (18%) are cited as the biggest barrier delaying AI agent adoption.
- **Investment outlook**: 84% of enterprise leaders say it's likely (48%) or certain (36%) their org will increase AI agent investments over the next 12 months. Only 2% are certain they won't increase investment; only 1% are actively decreasing funding.
- **Perceived potential**: 30% (close to 1 in 3) see the most potential for AI agents in automating routine workflows; 17% in improving customer experiences; 12% in strategy/decision-making assistance; only 5% say they see no potential.
- Article closes with a Zapier product pitch (build no-code agents via Zapier templates/Copilot, 9,000+ app integrations, enterprise security/compliance — access control, audit logs, agent activity monitoring, GDPR/SOC 2 Type II/CCPA).

## Definitions and terminology
- **Human-in-the-loop**: AI agents act, but approval gates are built into the workflow — a human reviews before an agent's output (e.g., a drafted email or data report) moves further down the pipeline. Framed in the article as the dominant, most cautious management style, contrasted with the smaller (20%) minimal-oversight/autonomous group.
- **Agentic AI adoption**: distinguished across three states — production (actively deployed and operating), pilot/testing, and planning (intend to adopt, not yet started).
