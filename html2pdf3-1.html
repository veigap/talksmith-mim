Objective

Industry 4.0 — driven by data, connectivity, and artificial intelligence — is changing how value is created and how decisions are made. For managers, AI is quickly becoming the most powerful lever available to improve judgment, speed, and execution, both at the company level and in their own day-to-day work.

This course takes a hands-on approach. You will work directly with state-of-the-art tools that leaders are already using to augment their teams and their decisions, and you will discuss the real challenges of adoption: data quality, cultural resistance, ethical trade-offs, and separating hype from value.

By the end of the program, you will leave with a clearer view of where AI creates real business value, a practical toolkit you can apply immediately, and a new way of thinking about your own productivity as an augmented leader.

Because the future will not belong to companies that simply use AI. It will belong to the managers who are augmented by it.

Industry 4.0 — The 8 Stages

Industry 4.0 is not a one-time project — it is a continuous journey of digital evolution. The eight stages below are the backbone of the course narrative; each class session maps to one or more of them.

Step Focus Goal
1 Assessment Know where you are — map current processes, technology, and digital maturity, identify gaps, and define baseline KPIs.
2 Strategy Know where you’re going — set a clear vision aligned with business goals, prioritize high-ROI use cases, and secure leadership buy-in.
3 Connectivity Connect everything — deploy IoT sensors, modern networks, and secure data platforms to capture reliable real-time data.
4 Integration Break data silos — integrate ERP, MES, and SCADA systems so information flows seamlessly across the value chain.
5 Analytics Turn data into insight — apply Big Data, AI, and ML to predict, optimize, and support better decisions.
6 Automation Let machines do more — use cobots, digital twins, and autonomous systems to boost efficiency, safety, and quality.
7 People Bring humans along — reskill teams, redesign workflows, and build a culture of innovation and continuous learning.
8 Scale Grow and improve — scale successful pilots, monitor KPIs, and evolve continuously with partners and new technologies.

DAY 6 — Continuous Improvement & Scaling: Orchestrating Agents with Paperclip ● PAPERCLIP

Sat Aug 29 · 8:30 – 12:00 · Presencial · 3.5 h

GOAL Take the delegation mindset one level up: instead of guiding a single agent, add a specialized agent to an existing organization of agents and steer it toward a goal — while a human stays firmly in the loop through approval gates and budgets. Meet Paperclip, an open-source framework for exactly that.
I4.0 PHASE Continuous Improvement & Scaling (Step 8)
TOOLS Paperclip (open-source agent orchestration)
SESSION FLOW → From one agent to an organization — recap the arc: Chat → delegating a result (Cowork) → orchestrating a team of agents toward a goal (25 min) → What Paperclip is — an open-source control plane that assigns goals to a hierarchy of AI agents (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor) with a human operator at the top; tour the pre-built organization (25 min) → Human in the loop, by design — approval gates for sensitive actions, per-agent token budgets (soft warning → hard stop), and a circuit breaker for abnormal spend: you watch progress against goals, not every call (25 min) → Mission — add and run a new specialized agent (35 min) → Debrief + course close — where this is heading and where human judgment still matters (10 min)
MISSION The organization already exists in Paperclip — your job is to add one new specialized agent: a “Director of Institutional Relations.” Give it a clear mandate: analyze the potential risks facing the institution and, as mitigation, produce awareness content — e.g. blog posts — that gets ahead of them. Wire it into the existing org, hand it the goal, set a token budget, and drive it by approving the key steps (which risks to prioritize, which content to publish). Success: the new agent produces a risk read plus at least one awareness blog draft, and every sensitive step passed through your approval.
TAKEAWAYS ✓ How to extend an existing agent organization with a new specialized role. ✓ How the delegation model scales from one agent to an orchestrated team. ✓ Why human-in-the-loop controls (approval gates, budgets, circuit breakers) are what make autonomy safe.

Appendix — The Atlas Mission (Days 1–2)

Days 1 and 2 — the Saturday double session — teach Claude Cowork through one running mission: Atlas, your team’s virtual market analyst. Your boss needs a weekly pulse on three companies — Apple (AAPL), Microsoft (MSFT), and Nvidia (NVDA) — delivered two ways: an email in the inbox once a week and an always-updated dashboard (a Live Artifact) he can open whenever he wants. Instead of building that report by hand every Monday, you build Atlas in Cowork to do it for you — without writing a single line of code.

Across Days 1–2 you grow Atlas through four milestones, each a working increment that unlocks a new Cowork building block on top of the last. The full brief lives in mission.md.

Milestone What Atlas achieves Cowork superpower unlocked
1 · 🥚 Atlas is born On-demand report: turns the intern’s scattered notes into a clean weekly pulse with a fixed format Projects · Instructions · Skills
2 · 🔎 Atlas learns to research Autonomous report: gathers news and prices on its own — no more manual copy-paste Tools (web search) · Connectors (market data)
3 · 🐣 Atlas works alone Ready-to-send report: every Monday it leaves a draft in Gmail, ready to review and send Connectors (Gmail) · Schedule
4 · 📣 Atlas delivers the dashboard Live dashboard: the pulso-semanal Live Artifact refreshes itself with the latest data every week, living in the Live artifacts tab Artifacts (Live Artifacts) · Skills
