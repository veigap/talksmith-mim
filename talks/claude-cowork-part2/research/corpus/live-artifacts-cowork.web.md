# Use live artifacts in Claude Cowork (Claude Help Center)

## Key claims
- Los Live Artifacts son "persistent, interactive HTML dashboards that Claude builds for you", con forma "shaped around your specific work".
- Existen sólo en la app de escritorio: "available on the desktop app only. They don't appear in the Artifacts view on web or mobile". Claude Desktop corre en macOS, Windows y Linux (beta), sólo en planes pagos (Pro, Max, Team, Enterprise).
- Al abrirse "pull fresh data from your connected apps"; un caché corto acelera la carga y el artifact "re-queries your connected apps on its own".
- Permisos: "Live artifacts use your connectors without asking" — "artifacts don't ask for permission before using connectors, even if your session mode would normally require approval". Sólo pueden usar "the connectors you approved during creation or update".
- Autenticación al compartir: "Shared artifacts use the viewer's access, not yours. When someone opens your artifact, it connects to their connectors and data sources".
- Versionado: "Each time you iterate on a live artifact with Claude, the previous version is saved"; se pueden restaurar versiones.
- Compartir: sólo "Team and Enterprise plans. On Pro and Max plans, live artifacts can't be shared or published". El compartir "stays within your organization. There are no external or public links".
- Localidad: "Live artifacts live on your computer. If you switch devices, they don't come with you."

## Definitions and terminology
- **Live Artifact:** página HTML persistente e interactiva que Cowork crea para un trabajo concreto y que refresca datos de las aplicaciones conectadas.
